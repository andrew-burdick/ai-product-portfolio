# Kestrel Business Bank: company profile (fictional)

Kestrel Business Bank is a fictional institution created for this portfolio. Any resemblance to a real bank is unintended.

## Snapshot
- Mid-sized Australian bank focused on small and medium businesses
- ~180,000 business customers; ~1,200 staff
- Segments: sole traders, micro businesses (fewer than 5 staff), SMEs (5–200 staff)
- Products: business transaction accounts, merchant payments, overdrafts and business loans, term deposits
- Channels: mobile app, internet banking, contact centre, 35 business banking centres, relationship bankers for larger SMEs

## Strategic priorities (next 18 months)
1. Grow the SME customer base by 10%
2. Increase market share through selective partnerships, such as with business formation and registration platforms
3. Cut new business account onboarding from 5 days to 1 day   

## Known pain points (hypotheses to test)
- Bankers lack confidence across the full product range, lowering cross-sell and causing avoidable handoffs to other bankers (measures: cross-sell rate; handoffs per enquiry)
- High contact-centre volumes on basic product and fee questions
- Slow, paperwork-heavy business account onboarding

## Constraints
- Regulated by APRA, ASIC and AUSTRAC; AML/CTF obligations apply
- Cautious risk appetite for customer-facing AI
- Legacy core banking systems; limited integration budget
- Limited access to and skill creating insights interfaces like Power BI

## AI maturity
- Analytics team exists but is stretched; limited self-serve reporting capability (e.g. Power BI) in frontline and product teams
- Internal productivity AI pilot underway
- No customer-facing generative AI in production
